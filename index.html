Warmth, stitched by hand
for those who need it.

We are a group of volunteers who crochet squares, join them together and add a border to make cosy rugs — given free to people living with illness, cancer, or the frailty of age.

Request a rug Join us / donate wool

A little warmth goes a long way

Every rug is made from dozens of hand-crocheted squares, each one stitched by a member of our group. There is no charge — just care, colour and comfort.

🧶

Made by hand

Our members crochet squares at home, then we gather to join them, add a border and finish each rug with love.

💗

Given with care

Rugs go to people facing illness or hardship — in hospitals, hospices, aged care and at home.

🤝

Powered by kindness

We run entirely on volunteers and donated wool. Every ball and every hour is a gift to someone in need.

How it works

From a single square to a finished rug in someone's hands.

Crochet

Members crochet squares in our standard size using donated wool.

Combine

Squares are joined together into a full-size rug.

Border

A neat border is added to finish and strengthen each rug.

Give

The completed rug is gifted to someone who needs comfort.

“The rug arrived the week Mum went into palliative care. She never let it go. Thank you for such a beautiful gift.” — a family we helped

Get involved

Whether you crochet, have wool to spare, or know someone who could use a little warmth — we'd love to hear from you.
